About the Clinic

Meet Dr. Anjali Verma

Dr. Anjali Verma (MBBS, MD Dermatology) founded X Medical to make dermatology care straightforward: a proper consultation, a diagnosis you understand, and a treatment plan that fits your routine — not a shelf of products you don't need.

  • MBBS, MD (Dermatology, Venereology & Leprosy)
  • 12+ years of clinical dermatology experience
  • Specialist in acne, pigmentation and hair loss management
  • Member, Indian Association of Dermatologists

How a visit works

Every first-time patient goes through the same simple process.

Step 1

Book your slot

Choose a convenient time through our online appointment form.

Consultation

A detailed skin/hair assessment and history review with Dr. Anjali Verma.
